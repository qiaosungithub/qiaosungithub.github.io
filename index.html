Selected Papers

Is Noise Conditioning Necessary for Denoising Generative Models?

Qiao Sun*, Zhicheng Jiang*, Hanhong Zhao*, Kaiming He

ICML 2025

Revisits whether diffusion and flow-matching models need noise conditioning, reimplementing eight denoising generative models across several datasets. Proposes uEDM, a noise-unconditional diffusion model with competitive performance, with theory that matches the observed behavior.

  • arXiv

Bidirectional Normalizing Flow: From Data to Noise and Back

Yiyang Lu*†, Qiao Sun*†, Xianbang Wang*, Zhicheng Jiang, Hanhong Zhao, Kaiming He

CVPR 2026 · Highlight

Revisits normalizing flows with a learned reverse map, removing explicit inverse-flow computation and slow autoregressive inference. Hidden-state alignment guides the reverse model, enabling single-evaluation generation with state-of-the-art results among normalizing flows.

  • arXiv

One-step Latent-free Image Generation with Pixel Mean Flows

Yiyang Lu*, Susie Lu*, Qiao Sun*, Hanhong Zhao*, Zhicheng Jiang, Xianbang Wang, Tianhong Li, Zhengyang Geng, Kaiming He

ICML 2026

A strong baseline for one-step, latent-free image generation in pixel space, using MeanFlow with x-prediction. Reaches 2.22 FID on ImageNet 256×256 and 2.48 FID on ImageNet 512×512.
